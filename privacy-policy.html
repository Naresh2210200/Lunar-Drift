Privacy Policy for Lunar Drift

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lunar Drift ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the game. Lunar Drift is a single-player endless-runner game.

1. Data We Collect Directly

Lunar Drift does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location) to our own servers. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

Lunar Drift shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to earn in-game currency) using Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device and app information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad performance)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. You can review how Google handles this data here: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how ads are personalized here: https://adssettings.google.com .

3. Children's Privacy

Lunar Drift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties ourselves. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, described above, which operates under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Data Retention & Your Choices

Since we don't collect personal data on our own servers, there is no developer-held data to request deletion of. To limit ad personalization/tracking at the device level, you can adjust your device's advertising ID settings in your phone's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, on most Android devices).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
